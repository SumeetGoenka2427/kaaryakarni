About Khandelwal Samaj, Jaipur

A community organization dedicated to the welfare, unity and progress of the Khandelwal families of Jaipur.

Our History

The Khandelwal Samaj of Jaipur has served the community for over four decades, providing a platform for members to connect, support one another and preserve shared traditions. What began as informal gatherings has grown into a structured organization offering welfare, education support and community services.

This membership portal continues that legacy by making Samaj services — membership, business networking, donations and community information — accessible online for every member.

Our Mission

To strengthen the Khandelwal community in Jaipur through membership services, welfare support and trustworthy business collaboration.

Our Vision

A connected, self-reliant Samaj where every member has access to community support, opportunity and shared cultural identity.

Our Values

Trust, service, unity and respect for tradition — guiding every initiative undertaken by the Samaj.
